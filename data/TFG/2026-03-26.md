The most useful off-stream prep is:

- Scout for clay and mark it, but avoid burning the good progression payoffs off stream if you want them live on stream.
- Learn the pit kiln recipe by heart:
  - place up to four items in a 1x1 hole
  - add 8 straw

  - a jug
  - a saw blade mold
- Pick up and mark any surface small copper you find. Small ore pieces are both early metal and indicators of a nearby vein, but each one only gives 10 mB, so you need 100 mB total for a first cast.

## Practical TFG Notes

- In TerraFirmaCraft, clay is often hidden by grass.
- Clay indicators include Athyrium Fern, Canna, Goldenrod, Pampas Grass, Perovskia, and Water Canna.
- Smaller clay deposits can also show up near rivers, lakes, and ponds.
- A pit kiln is the intended early-game way to fire clay, and it burns for about six in-game hours.
- The clean early progression path in TerraFirmaGreg's Stone Age quest flow is:

`clay -> vessel / jug / saw mold -> first copper saw -> lumber -> workbench -> pickaxe / mining prep`

## Suggested Stream Plan

### Session 1: Clay Unlock

Goals:

- Open by showing what was learned off stream: clay indicators, why clay matters, and how pit kilns work.
- Find or return to clay.
- Fire a small vessel, a jug, and a saw blade mold.
- Use the jug to solve the thirst problem properly.
- If time allows, make a little extra pottery for storage.

Why this works:

- It directly resolves the bottleneck identified at the end of the stream.
- It turns abstract confusion into visible progress quickly.

### Session 2: First Metal and First Saw

Goals:

- Use the small vessel plus pit kiln to melt enough early copper.
- Cast the saw blade mold into the first saw.
- Turn that into lumber, then a workbench.
- If pace is good, make a quern and handstone as a side objective.

Why this works:

- In the pack's actual quest flow, the first saw is a real milestone.
- Saw -> lumber -> workbench is the point where the run starts feeling more structured and less improvised.

### Session 3: First Mining Prep

Goals:

- Make the next mold that matters, usually the pickaxe mold.
- Get the first pickaxe online.
- Prepare to mine properly with support beams and ladders.
- If available, add a propick rather than blind digging.

Why this works:

- It follows naturally from the clay and copper work.
- It keeps progression clean instead of bouncing between unrelated systems.

### Session 4: Survival Competence

Goals:

- Revisit food, thirst, wetness, and temperature once containers and tools are in better shape.
- Build a more stable home loop around water, storage, shelter, and cooking.
- If the stream still has momentum, move toward soups and better nutrition management.

Why this works:

- The first stream showed that weather, thirst, and general survival friction were major sources of uncertainty.
- This session turns that uncertainty into confidence instead of panic.

## Recommendation

- know where clay is
